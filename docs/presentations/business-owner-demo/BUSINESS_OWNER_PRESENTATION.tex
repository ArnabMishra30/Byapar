% ---------- Part 1 ----------
\section{Part 1 — Opening \dur{2 min}}
\begin{say}
``In your shop today, how do you track things? Most owners juggle a few registers:
one for bills, one for \emph{udhaar}, a notebook for stock, supplier dues in their
head. That works\ldots until a bill book gets lost, or you forget who owes you
\rupee2,000. Byapar puts all of it in \textbf{one place} — you make a bill, and your
stock, the customer's balance and your reports update by themselves.''
\end{say}

% ---------- Part 2 ----------
\section{Part 2 — Dashboard \dur{2 min}}
\begin{flow}
\ui{Home} & The day's summary, quick shortcuts, and the grouped sidebar. \\
Sidebar & Sales, Purchases, Stock, Parties, Money, Reports — ``grouped the way you think.'' \\
\end{flow}
\begin{say}
``This is your shop's home screen — your day the moment you log in. On the left is
everything, grouped the way you think about your shop.''
\end{say}
\shot{02-dashboard.png}{The Home dashboard.}

% ---------- Part 3 ----------
\section{Part 3 — Products \& Inventory \dur{3 min}}
\begin{flow}
\ui{Products / Items} & The catalogue, each item with a purchase and selling price. \\
\ui{Add product} & \emph{Sunflower Oil 1L}, purchase \rupee115, selling \rupee140, min stock 10. \\
\ui{Stock Dashboard} & Live quantity + value; goes up on buying, down on selling. \\
\ui{Low Stock} & Items at/below their minimum — your re‑order list. \\
\end{flow}
\begin{say}
``Each item has a buy price and a sell price. This `minimum stock' flags the item in
Low Stock before you run out. And Stock Dashboard here is never counted by hand —
selling reduces it, buying increases it, automatically.''
\end{say}
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white,colframe=panelborder,boxrule=0.8pt,arc=3pt,left=9pt,right=9pt,top=6pt,bottom=6pt,
  fonttitle=\sffamily\bfseries\color{navy},title={Worked example},enhanced]
\textbf{Sunflower Oil 1L} — opening stock 50. Buy 20 $\to$ \textbf{70}. Sell 5 $\to$ \textbf{65}.
You never change the number; the purchase and the sale do.
\end{tcolorbox}

% ---------- Part 4 ----------
\section{Part 4 — Unified Parties \& Credit \dur{3 min} \textbf{\textcolor{brandgreen}{$\star$ key}}}
\begin{flow}
\ui{All Parties} & One list of everyone; ``Owes you'' and ``You owe'' columns. \\
\ui{XYZ Traders} $\to$ Statement & Running \textbf{receivable} and \textbf{payable}, kept apart. \\
\ui{Credit Book} & Everyone who owes you, to follow up. \\
\end{flow}
\begin{say}
``There are no separate customer and supplier lists — one list of \textbf{parties},
because the same business often does both. Take \textbf{XYZ Traders}: they
\textbf{owe you \rupee6,000} on sales, and \textbf{you owe them \rupee15,000} on
purchases. Byapar keeps these two \textbf{separate} — it does not mix them into one
number. You always know exactly where you stand.''
\end{say}
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white,colframe=panelborder,boxrule=0.8pt,arc=3pt,enhanced,
  left=9pt,right=9pt,top=5pt,bottom=5pt]
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}X r@{}}
Sold \rupee8,000, received \rupee2,000 & \textbf{Owes You \rupee6,000} \\
Bought \rupee20,000, paid \rupee5,000 & \textbf{You Owe \rupee15,000} \\
\end{tabularx}
\end{tcolorbox}

% ---------- Part 5 ----------
\section{Part 5 — Quick Billing \& Sales \dur{3 min}}
\begin{flow}
\ui{Quick Billing} & Choose party \emph{Rahul Store}. \\
Add items & Oil $\times$2, Soap $\times$3, Maggi $\times$5 — prices auto‑fill. \\
Payment & \ui{Full} / \ui{Part} / \ui{On credit}, and the account it goes into. \\
\ui{Save \& complete} & Invoice + stock out + payment, in one click. \\
\end{flow}
\begin{say}
``Making a bill takes seconds. Pick the party, add the items — the price fills in by
itself. Choose how it's paid. One press of \textbf{Save \& complete} just did four
things: made the invoice, took the items out of stock, recorded the payment, and put
any credit on the customer's account.''
\end{say}
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=panel,colframe=panelborder,boxrule=0.6pt,arc=3pt,left=9pt,right=9pt,top=5pt,bottom=5pt]
\small Line maths (GST off): 2$\times$140 = \rupee280, 3$\times$55 = \rupee165, 5$\times$14 = \rupee70 $\to$ \textbf{\rupee515}.
\end{tcolorbox}
\shot{08-quick-billing.png}{Quick Billing — party, items, payment panel, complete.}

% ---------- Part 6 ----------
\section{Part 6 — Purchases \& Money \dur{2 min}}
\begin{flow}
\ui{New purchase} & Supplier + items at purchase rate; complete $\to$ stock up, payable up. \\
\ui{Money Received} & Type the amount once; it settles the oldest bills. \\
\ui{Money Paid} & The same, for paying suppliers. \\
\end{flow}
\begin{say}
``Buying is the mirror of selling — stock goes up, and what you didn't pay becomes
what you owe, under \textbf{Payables}. Money Received applies a customer's payment to
their oldest bills automatically; Money Paid does the same going out.''
\end{say}

% ---------- Part 7 ----------
\section{Part 7 — AI Bill Import \dur{2 min}}
\begin{flow}
\ui{Bill Import} $\to$ Upload & Photograph a printed or neat handwritten bill. \\
Review & Byapar reads party, items, quantities, rates, discount, totals. \\
Confirm & Only then is it saved as a purchase or sale. \\
\end{flow}
\begin{say}
``Instead of typing a supplier's bill, photograph it — Byapar reads it and shows you
everything to check. You fix anything misread and confirm. \textbf{You always review
before confirming}; nothing is saved until you say so.''
\end{say}
\begin{warn}
\textbf{Test this the morning of.} If bill reading isn't enabled where you demo, show
the upload/review screens and call it ``a capability we enable for your shop'' —
\textbf{never fake a live read}.
\end{warn}

% ---------- Part 8 ----------
\section{Part 8 — Reports \& Statements \dur{2 min}}
\begin{flow}
\ui{Reports} tabs & Sales, Purchases, Stock, \textbf{Parties}, Profit \& Loss. \\
Date range + Download & Any period, as \textbf{PDF} or \textbf{Excel}. \\
Party $\to$ Statement $\to$ PDF & Each party's account, ready to send. \\
\end{flow}
\begin{say}
``At month‑end, no adding up by hand. Sales, purchases, stock, a single Parties view,
and a simple Profit \& Loss — any date range, downloaded as PDF or Excel for you, your
accountant or the bank.''
\end{say}
\shot{14-reports.png}{Reports — pick a tab and a date range, then print or download.}

% ---------- Part 9 ----------
\section{Part 9 — Closing \dur{1 min}}
\begin{say}
``So, in one place: faster billing, stock you can trust, one record per party with
credit on both sides, your full purchase and sales history, money in and out, and
reports you can download any time — with far less manual work.\\[4pt]
\textbf{Which part of your current day takes the most time, or causes the most
headache? Let's look at exactly how Byapar would handle that for you.}''
\end{say}

% ---------- Positioning ----------
\section{Positioning (your back pocket)}
\subsection{30 seconds}
``Byapar is a simple app to run the money side of your shop — billing, stock, who owes
you, who you owe, and reports — all in one place, on your phone or computer.''
\subsection{Five benefits}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.3em,itemsep=1pt]
\item Bills in seconds — stock, credit and reports update automatically.
\item One unified party record — customer and supplier, never mixed up.
\item Receivables and payables tracked separately and clearly.
\item Live stock with low‑stock alerts.
\item Download‑ready reports and statements (PDF \& Excel).
\end{enumerate}
\subsection{Five differentiators (all real)}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.3em,itemsep=1pt]
\item \textbf{Unified Parties} — one person, both sides, balances kept apart.
\item \textbf{AI Bill Import} — photograph a bill; Byapar reads it for you to review.
\item \textbf{Server‑calculated money} — totals and balances come from the books, always exact.
\item \textbf{Auto payment allocation} — a receipt settles the oldest bills on its own.
\item \textbf{One‑click Save \& complete} — a bill posts stock, invoice and payment together.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Likely questions — short answers}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}>{\bfseries\color{navy}}p{6.0cm}X@{}}
\toprule
Better than a notebook? & A notebook can't total your stock, remember credit, or make a report. Byapar does, while you just record the sale. \\
Track credit customers? & Yes — ``On credit''/``Part'' on the bill; shows in the Credit Book. \\
Same party buy \& sell? & Yes — one record, two balances kept separate. \\
See what I owe? & Yes — ``You owe'' and the Payables screen (which bills are due). \\
Track stock / auto‑update? & Yes — live stock; sales reduce it, purchases add to it. \\
Upload handwritten bills? & Yes if reading is enabled; review before confirming. \\
Download Excel? & Yes — reports and statements as PDF \& Excel. \\
Multiple employees? & Yes — owner full access; staff can be limited; cashiers see only their own sales. \\
Use without GST? & Yes — bills then carry no tax. \\
If I enter something wrong? & Fix a draft before completing; correct a completed one with a return or adjustment. \\
Access my data later? & Yes — saved securely; available when you sign in. \\
Subscription / price? & Per‑business subscription — \emph{share your current plan, don't quote from memory}. \\
No internet? & It's an online app; reconnect and carry on — data is safe on the server. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
