# Byapar Demo — Pre‑Meeting Checklist

Run through this **the morning of**, and again **5 minutes before** you start.

## Connection & environment
- [ ] **Internet** is working and stable (Byapar is an online app).
- [ ] The app **opens** at your demo address and the **backend is up** (the dashboard loads, no "Cannot reach the server").
- [ ] You can **log in** with your demo **Shop Owner** account (not a staff/cashier account — you want full access).
- [ ] Browser is a **clean desktop window**: close unrelated tabs, hide bookmarks bar, no personal logins visible.
- [ ] Zoom level is readable on the meeting screen/projector.

## Demo data is present
- [ ] **Products** exist (Sunflower Oil 1L, Dove Soap 100g, Maggi Noodles 70g, Coca‑Cola 750ml).
- [ ] **Parties** exist (XYZ Traders, Rahul Store, ABC Distributors) and at least one has **both** an "Owes you" and a "You owe" balance (XYZ Traders).
- [ ] **Stock Dashboard** shows quantities and a stock value.
- [ ] **Credit Book / Payables** show at least a few open balances.

## Each flow works (do a dry run)
- [ ] **Quick Billing** — pick a party, add an item (price auto‑fills), take payment, **Save & complete** → an invoice appears.
- [ ] **New Purchase** — pick a supplier, add an item, complete → stock goes up.
- [ ] **Money Received** — open the dialog, amount applies to open bills.
- [ ] **Money Paid** — opens and works.
- [ ] **Reports** — Sales, Stock and **Parties** tabs load; a **date range** works.
- [ ] **PDF download** works (party statement or a report).
- [ ] **Excel download** works.

## AI Bill Import (test it live first)
- [ ] Bill reading is **enabled** on the app you're demoing (upload a sample bill and confirm it **reads**).
- [ ] If it does **not** read, decide now: show the **upload + review screens** and call it "a capability we enable for your shop" — **do not fake** a live read.
- [ ] A **sample printed bill** is ready (image/PDF on the machine).
- [ ] A **sample handwritten bill** is ready (neat).

## Safety net
- [ ] A **backup PDF** of this walkthrough and the **screenshots** are saved locally, in case the live app has a hiccup.
- [ ] You know the **teaching numbers** by heart: XYZ Traders ₹6,000 owes you / ₹15,000 you owe; Rahul Store bill ₹515.
- [ ] No **real customer data, phone numbers, or credentials** are visible on screen — demo data only.

## Mindset
- [ ] Open with **their problems**, not features.
- [ ] If asked something you're unsure of: *"Let me confirm and come back to you."* (See `KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md`.)
- [ ] End with the question: *"Which part of your day takes the most time?"*
