> lists. There is **one list of people you deal with** — we call them *parties*. Why?

**Open:** click **XYZ Traders** → open their **Statement**.

> "You sell them goods, *and* you also buy from them. Byapar keeps both sides on one

>
> - You sold them ₹8,000 of goods, they paid ₹2,000 — so they **owe you ₹6,000**.
> - You bought ₹20,000 from them, you paid ₹5,000 — so **you owe them ₹15,000**.

> ₹15,000 still has to go out. You always know exactly where you stand with every
> party. And this statement is their complete history — every sale, purchase and
> payment, in date order — which you can download as PDF or Excel and send on
> WhatsApp."

**Open:** `Parties → Credit Book`.

> "And this is your whole *udhaar* book, kept for you — everyone who owes you, at a

# Product positioning (keep in your back pocket)

**30‑second intro:**
> "Byapar is a simple app to run the money side of your shop — billing, stock, who
> owes you, who you owe, and reports — all in one place, on your phone or computer."

**1‑minute explanation:**
> "You make a bill in seconds and the stock, the customer's balance and your reports
> update by themselves. Suppliers, customers and *udhaar* all live in one place. At
> month‑end you download your sales, profit and party statements instead of adding
> them up by hand. It works with or without GST."

**2‑minute business‑value pitch:**
> "Most shops lose money in small leaks — forgotten credit, stock that ran out,
> bills that went missing, hours spent at month‑end. Byapar closes those leaks. Every
> sale and purchase is recorded once and updates everything. You always know who owes
> you and what you owe. You see what's running low before it's gone. And your records
> are ready whenever your accountant or a lender asks. It's the difference between
> running your shop from memory and running it from numbers you can trust."

**5 key benefits:**
1. Bills in seconds — stock, credit and reports update automatically.
2. One unified party record — customer and supplier, never mixed up.
3. Receivables and payables tracked separately and clearly.
4. Live stock with low‑stock alerts.
5. Download‑ready reports and party statements (PDF & Excel).

**5 differentiators (all real, implemented):**
1. **Unified Parties** — one person can be customer *and* supplier; balances kept apart.
2. **AI Bill Import** — photograph a bill; Byapar reads it for you to review.
3. **Server‑calculated money** — totals, tax and balances come from the books, so figures are always exact.
4. **Auto payment allocation** — a receipt settles the oldest bills on its own.
5. **One‑click Save & complete** — a bill posts stock, the invoice and the payment together.

# Questions a business owner may ask (verified answers)

1. **How is this better than a notebook?**
   A notebook can't add up your stock, remind you who owes you, or make a report. Byapar does all three while you simply record the sale.
2. **Can I track customers who buy on credit?**
   Yes — choose "On credit" or "Part" on the bill; the balance shows in the Credit Book until paid.
3. **Can the same party buy from me and sell to me?**
   Yes. One party record holds both sides, and the two balances stay separate.
4. **Can I see how much I owe someone?**
   Yes — "You owe" on the party, and the **Payables** screen shows which bills are due.
5. **Can I track stock?**
   Yes — a live Stock Dashboard, with Low Stock alerts.
6. **Will stock update automatically after sales and purchases?**
   Yes — selling reduces stock, buying increases it, with no manual entry.
7. **Can I upload handwritten bills?**
   Yes, if bill reading is enabled for your shop; neat handwriting reads best — and you always review before confirming.
8. **Can I download reports in Excel?**
   Yes — reports and party statements download as PDF and Excel.
9. **Can multiple employees use it?**
   Yes — the owner has full access; staff/cashier accounts can be limited (e.g. billing only), and cashiers see only their own sales.
10. **Can I use it without GST?**
    Yes — leave GST off and bills carry no tax.
11. **What if I enter something incorrectly?**
    Fix a draft before completing it. A completed bill is final on purpose — you correct it with a return or a stock adjustment, which keeps a clean trail.
12. **Can I access my business information later?**
    Yes — it's saved securely and available whenever you sign in.
13. **How does subscription/payment work?**  *(confirm your current plan/pricing before quoting — see KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md)*
    Byapar runs on a subscription per business; share your current plan details rather than guessing on the spot.
14. **What happens if I lose internet?**
    Byapar is an online app, so you need a connection to record and view. When you're back online, carry on — your data is safe on the server. *(It is not an offline app today.)*

> that and come back to you,"* rather than promising. See `KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md`.
